# INC-9101 — Second payment to the same vendor refused as "PIX duplicado"

**Reported by:** Contas a Pagar (two analysts), integrations team
**Severity:** high — payments are being delayed and analysts are typing keys by hand as a workaround

## What users see

- A vendor (a cleaning company) prints a PIX QR code on every monthly invoice. The QR is always the same picture; their bank calls it a "QR estático".
- Their invoices reach us through the invoice-intake integration, which reads the QR from the PDF and creates the título through the API with the QR's copia e cola in the installment's `pixPayloadEmv`.
- March's título was created fine.
- April's invoice, same vendor, same QR: the API refuses the título with `409` *"This PIX Copia e Cola is already registered on 1043 (Higienizadora …)"*. The March título is still open (it is scheduled, not paid yet).
- An invoice billed in two installments, both payable with that same QR, is refused with *"The same PIX Copia e Cola is on more than one installment of this request."*
- The integrations team sees the same refusal (`requisition.pix_payload_emv_duplicate`) when it fills the payment plan of a solicitação for that vendor.
- Workaround in use: an analyst decodes the QR by hand and creates the título with the vendor's PIX key typed in. That works, but the QR is lost and analysts make typos.

## What still behaves

- When the vendor sends a QR that is generated per invoice (a cobrança with its own link), reusing it on a second título is refused. Treasury confirmed that refusal is correct and wants to keep it.

## Not yet known

- Whether this affects every vendor that uses a fixed QR, or only some banks' QRs.
