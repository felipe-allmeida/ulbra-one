# INC-5101 — Colleague shown on a requisition for hours after leaving it

Severity: S3 · Reported by: Purchasing (Canoas) · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
Two buyers work the same requisition. Buyer A opens it, then navigates to the payables list inside the
app (her browser tab stays open). Buyer B keeps seeing A's avatar on the requisition for the rest of
the afternoon. The avatar disappears only when A closes her browser. Reloading B's page does not help.

Not reproducible every time. First reports came the week we raised `api_max_replicas` from 1 to 2.

## What operations observed
- Access log, 14:02 (two replicas, `api--a` and `api--b`; A's stream is held by `api--a`):
  - `14:02:11.096 api--b POST   /api/realtime/presence  204  (5 ms)`
  - `14:02:11.241 api--a DELETE /api/realtime/presence  204  (4 ms)`
- During the same minute `api--b` was CPU-throttled; its Redis client showed command latency p99 of
  1.3 s (`api--a`: 2 ms).
- Redis `MONITOR`, filtered on the requisition's presence key, after 14:02:11:
  - `ZREM` of A's member (from `api--a`), then `ZADD` of A's member (from `api--b`, about 1.2 s later)
  - from then on, a `ZADD` of A's member every 20 s from `api--a`, for as long as A's tab stayed open
- No errors or warnings in the API logs for that window.
