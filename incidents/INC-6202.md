# INC-6202 — Re-approved titles: provision missing after classification, liability goes negative after payment

Severity: S2 · Reported by: Contabilidade + Tesouraria · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
Since requisition titles can be returned (devolução) and re-approved, Contabilidade sees two related problems.

1. **Returned titles that had been reclassified.** These keep their expense in the DRE while they are back
   in Budgeting being corrected. They are not paid and not scheduled at that point.
2. **Titles re-approved without a classification.** A corrected requisition comes back into Finance without an
   expense account. Its provision shows up as a pending posting ("conta não classificada"), as expected.
   Contábil then classifies the title, and everything looks fine on screen. But:
   - the provision never appears in the Livro Razão;
   - the pending posting for that title stays open forever on the pending list;
   - when Tesouraria pays the title, Fornecedores a Pagar ends up with a **debit** balance for it (a negative
     liability), because the payment entry has no provision to offset.

## Example (company Gravataí, NF 7730, R$ 800,00)
- 02/09: scheduled with account 3.3.01.01.001. The provision is posted.
- 04/09: returned to the requester. Its provision is reversed.
- 08/09: corrected and re-approved. It arrives unclassified, and an open pending posting is created.
- 09/09: Contábil classifies it as 3.3.01.02.004. No Razão entry appears, and the pending posting stays open.
- 15/09: paid R$ 800,00. Fornecedores a Pagar for this title now shows a debit of R$ 800,00.

No errors are logged by any accounting consumer during these steps.
