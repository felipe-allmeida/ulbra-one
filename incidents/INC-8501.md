# INC-8501 — Colleague shown on a requisition long after leaving it

Severity: S3 · Reported by: Compras · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
Buyers see colleagues' avatars on requisitions they left a long time ago — sometimes for the rest of the day.
It goes away when that colleague closes the browser tab. It happens mostly on deploy days, and on Monday morning
when the Wi-Fi in building 14 was unstable.

## What we observed
- For one case, the colleague's browser (HAR file) shows `DELETE /api/realtime/presence?...` at 10:41:07 failing
  with `502 Bad Gateway` (the replica was draining), and no other DELETE for that requisition afterwards. The tab
  stayed open and the user kept working in other screens of the app.
- API logs: the colleague's presence on that requisition is renewed every 20 s until 17:58, when the tab closed.
