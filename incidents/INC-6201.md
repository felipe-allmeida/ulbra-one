# INC-6201 — Cancelled titles still show up as expenses and as amounts owed

Severity: S2 (affects the monthly close) · Reported by: Contabilidade · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
While closing September, Contabilidade found that the trial balance (balancete) and the DRE still show
expenses for titles Finance had cancelled. Fornecedores a Pagar (2.2.05.15.080) also keeps a credit balance
for those same titles, as if they were still owed. Finance confirms they are cancelled and will never be paid.

The accountants noticed one thing all the affected titles have in common: each was reclassified by
Contábil (its expense account was corrected) before it was cancelled. Cancelled titles that were never
reclassified look right.

## Example (company Canoas, NF 48211, R$ 1.000,00)
Livro Razão entries for this title, in order:

| # | Data | Histórico | Débito | Crédito |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01/09 | Provisão título a pagar | 3.3.01.01.001 Material de consumo 1.000,00 | 2.2.05.15.080 Fornecedores a Pagar 1.000,00 |
| 2 | 03/09 | Estorno — Reclassificação contábil | 2.2.05.15.080 1.000,00 | 3.3.01.01.001 1.000,00 |
| 3 | 03/09 | Provisão título a pagar | 3.3.01.02.004 Material de informática 1.000,00 | 2.2.05.15.080 1.000,00 |

On 05/09 the title was cancelled in Finance. No new entry appeared in the Razão for it. The cancellation
consumer processed the `PayableCancelled` event successfully: no errors and no retries in the logs.

Balances for this title after the cancellation: 3.3.01.02.004 has a debit of R$ 1.000,00, and Fornecedores a Pagar has a credit of R$ 1.000,00.
