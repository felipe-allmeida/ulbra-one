# INC-7301 — Bell badge and avatars stuck right after the page loads

Severity: S3 · Reported by: Suporte (several users) · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
- "I logged in, the bell said 0, a colleague sent me a requisition seconds later and the bell stayed at 0 — it
  only changed about five minutes later." It happens mostly right after login or a full page reload, rarely later.
- On the requisition screen, a colleague who opened the same requisition while my screen was still loading never
  shows up in the avatars. If they arrive a few seconds after my screen finished loading, they show up fine.
- "I marked a notification as read while the page was still loading and the badge kept the old number."

## What we observed
- The SSE stream is connected (DevTools → EventStream shows the `notification` / `presence` events arriving).
- For the stuck cases, DevTools Network shows only ONE request for the count/presence after the event — the one
  that was already in flight when the event arrived — and no request after it.
- Waiting long enough always fixes it (the bell has a 5-minute safety refetch), or reloading the page.
