# INC-5103 — Avatars linger after every deploy

Severity: S4 · Reported by: Several teams · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
Right after each deploy, requisition screens show avatars of people who are not there anymore. They
vanish by themselves after about 45 seconds. The people who were "left behind" usually reappear a
moment later as themselves (their tab reconnects to another replica and re-enters).

## What operations observed
- Every occurrence lines up with a rolling deploy (Container Apps revision swap: the old replica gets
  SIGTERM, drains, exits within its 30 s grace period).
- Redis keeps the drained replica's members until their scores expire; nothing removes them earlier.
- Viewers on the surviving replica receive no `presence` signal when the old replica exits; their screen
  only changes on its next refetch.
