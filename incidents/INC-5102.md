# INC-5102 — "I reopened the requisition but nobody sees me"

Severity: S3 · Reported by: Budget approvers · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
An approver leaves a requisition and comes straight back to it (browser back button, or clicking it
again in the inbox within a second or two). Colleagues on the requisition no longer see her avatar,
and keep not seeing it while she works on it. It comes back only after she reloads the page.

Intermittent; seems to happen more when the platform is under load.

## What operations observed
- Access log for one occurrence (her stream is held by `api--a`):
  - `09:41:52.310 api--b DELETE /api/realtime/presence  204  (3 ms)`
  - `09:41:52.655 api--a POST   /api/realtime/presence  204  (4 ms)`
- `api--b` Redis client latency p99 was 1.1 s at that moment.
- `GET /api/realtime/presence` for the requisition, on both replicas, does not include her for the next
  20 minutes (sampled every minute), although her stream stayed connected the whole time.
