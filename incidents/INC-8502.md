# INC-8502 — "I went back to the requisition and nobody sees me"

Severity: S3 · Reported by: Aprovadores · Status: open, not diagnosed

## What users see
An approver opens a requisition, goes back to the inbox, and opens the same requisition again (same tab, no
reload). The second time, colleagues on the requisition do not see her avatar, and it never appears while she
works on it. Reloading the page fixes it.

## What we observed
- HAR for one occurrence: `POST /api/realtime/presence` → 204 (first open), `DELETE` → 204 (back to inbox),
  `POST` → 204 (opened again). All three succeeded.
- API debug log for the third call: "presence command discarded (stale seq)" for her connection and that
  requisition.
